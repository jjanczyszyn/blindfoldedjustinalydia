An invitation

An evening crafted to wake the other senses.

We host intimate gatherings designed to slow the world down. Guests are gently led into a candle-lit room, blindfolded, and guided through a sequence of small, deliberate moments — the warmth of a hand, the weight of a glass, an aroma that surprises you, a flavour that suddenly feels new.

Conversation softens. Time loosens. What's on the table is a secret — and that's the point. When sight quiets, everything else gets louder.

The arc of the evening

The Journey

Every gathering follows the same gentle architecture. The details — what you'll taste, hear and feel — stay between us and the night.

  1. I

    Arrival

    We meet at golden hour. A short grounding ritual outdoors — breath, presence, the trees, a single shared note — settles the body before the senses are asked to listen more closely.

  2. II

    The Threshold

    Guests are welcomed into a softly-lit room dressed for the evening: candlelight, slow music, warm scent. A small ceremony marks the moment the blindfold goes on — and the night truly begins.

  3. III

    The Surrender

    With sight set aside, the room becomes a landscape of texture and temperature, weight and warmth. Each guest is guided, gently, by your hosts — never rushed, never alone in the dark.

  4. IV

    The Tasting

    A sequence of carefully composed courses unfolds, one act at a time. Each one is an invitation: feel before you decide, smell before you name, listen to your own surprise. The rest is for you to discover.

  5. V

    The Reveal

    Slowly, light returns. Blindfolds come off. We share what was felt, what was guessed, what was unexpected. Most guests describe leaving the table differently than they arrived.

The cuisine

Gourmet. Seasonal. Always considered.

The menu is a secret — that's part of the magic — but never an accident. Every course is composed with care: thoughtful sourcing, seasonal produce, and dishes designed to be experienced, not just eaten.

Dietary needs and allergies are gathered confidentially before each evening and woven into the menu so every guest is fully held. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, allergies — tell us, and we plan around you.
